Browsing the website

The supplied website does not add analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, local storage or session storage. Its illustrations and fonts are served locally or provided by your device. The walkthrough does not access your camera or any restaurant system.

Website hosting necessarily processes connection information, such as an IP address, to serve requests. Hosting provider: {{HOSTING}} {{HOSTING_LINK}} The provider’s processing and retention are separate from the email enquiry policy below.

Preparing an enquiry

The name, restaurant, city and optional message you type remain in the page while you prepare a draft. They are not submitted to a website server by this form. You can clear them using Clear form after preparing a draft. Your browser may have its own autofill or page-restoration behaviour.

Copy draft writes the draft to your device clipboard only when you select that button. Open my email app passes the draft to your email application. You must review and send it there. The website cannot see whether the message was sent or delivered.

If you send an email

The recipient and email providers process your email address and any details you include so we can respond to your enquiry and discuss a demonstration. Please share only what is necessary. Do not send customer records, access tokens, passwords or payment details.

Enquiry retention: {{RETENTION}} Please contact the operator to ask about access, correction or deletion of your enquiry. A pilot involving additional information will need a separate agreed notice and process.

What this notice does not cover

The private application demo, future restaurant service and any third-party email or hosting service have their own data flows. This static presentation does not activate guest account verification or collect phone numbers for SMS.
