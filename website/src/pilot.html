This page describes the prototype and a possible next step. It is not a restaurant service agreement.

What we can demonstrate

The working application includes table QR joining, individual orders, shared-item consent, exact individual shares and separate guest, waiter, manager and private platform-administration interfaces. Optional customer accounts use local TEST email in the existing demo.

What the demonstration does not do

Payments and POS connections are TEST simulators. No real card, MIA, bank, terminal or fiscal integration exists. Do not use real money, payment details or customer data in the demonstration.

Reservation exercises are fictional. They do not reserve capacity or book a real restaurant table. This presentation website cannot place an order, scan a live table QR, register restaurant staff or activate restaurant access.

Before a restaurant pilot

  • Agree the specific problem, restaurant workflow, participants and success measures.
  • Choose whether the trial is a fictional practice session or a separately approved operational pilot.
  • Confirm access permissions, staff training, privacy responsibilities and a fallback to the existing restaurant process.
  • Review hosting, backups, restoration and support arrangements.
  • Implement and validate any required real payment, POS or fiscal connections before relying on them.

Access and pricing

The full fictional demo is arranged privately. An enquiry is not automatic acceptance, a subscription or an obligation to pay. Pilot timing, scope and commercial terms must be agreed separately. Restaurant pricing has not been finalised.

Built with feedback, not assumptions

We have not yet validated outcomes in a live restaurant. A controlled pilot should measure the actual table experience and staff workload. We do not promise a particular revenue increase, time saving or compatibility with your current POS.
